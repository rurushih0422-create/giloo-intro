Introducing Giloo

A curated home for meaningful cinema

Giloo is a Taiwan-based curation platform for independent and art-house cinema, from festival titles and animation to issue-driven work. We connect these films with the right audiences across Asia, and help filmmakers find their audiences here. But Giloo is more than a large film library.

Giloo 是來自台灣的獨立與藝術電影策展平台，片單涵蓋影展作品、動畫到議題性作品。我們在亞洲替這些電影找到對的觀眾，也替創作者找到觀眾。Giloo 不只是一個很大的片庫。

What the Data Showed

Many people assume audiences only want short videos and quick content.

But on Giloo, some of our most-watched films deal with serious social issues, cultural trauma, and communities that are often unseen.

People still watch serious stories.

很多人以為觀眾只想看短影音。但在 Giloo，觀看數最高的一批片，講的是嚴肅的社會議題、文化創傷，以及平常沒被看見的群體。認真的故事，還是有人看。

Island in Between

金門 · 2023 · dir. S. Leo Chiang · Academy Award nominee, Best Documentary Short

金門是台灣與中國之間的小島。導演回到這裡，重新看自己的身分、家庭，以及台灣在世界上的位置。這是台灣第一部入圍奧斯卡的紀錄片。

The Problem

Through our work with filmmakers, festivals, and audiences, one problem became clear.

It is not a content problem. It is a distribution problem.

The films exist. The audiences exist. The path between them is broken.

和創作者、影展、觀眾工作這麼多年，我們看到一個清楚的問題。片子有，觀眾也有，斷掉的是中間那條路。

giloo
After the Screening

Festivals gather an audience, but after the festival, everyone goes home.

Theaters sell a ticket, and the relationship often ends when the audience leaves.

On a platform, audiences can build an ongoing relationship with filmmakers. They can follow a filmmaker, join a conversation, and come back for the next film.

But filmmakers still need a simple way to reach audiences beyond a single screening.

影展聚集觀眾，散場後大家各自回家。戲院賣一張票，關係常常就到走出戲院為止。在平台上，觀眾可以追蹤創作者、加入討論、等下一部作品。創作者需要的，是一個能走出單場放映的簡單方法。

AI Needs Context

Giloo's AI is built on what we already know: how audiences watch, how our partners work, what people write about films, and what we have learned from years of issue-based programming.

This helps AI connect films with the right audiences. It supports distribution, and filmmakers stay in control.

每個人都在談 AI，但 AI 需要脈絡。Giloo 的 AI 建立在我們已經知道的事情上：觀眾怎麼看片、夥伴怎麼工作、大家怎麼寫電影，以及多年議題策展累積的經驗。它幫忙把電影接上對的觀眾，決定權留在創作者手上。

giloo
Creative Control

AI works on distribution. You stay in control.

  • It does not replace curators, filmmakers, or communities. 它不會取代策展人、創作者或社群。
  • It may offer strategic insights, and the final decisions are always yours. 它可以給策略建議，最終決定永遠是你的。
  • You keep full control over your rights, your pricing, and every choice you make. 版權、定價、每一個選擇，都完全在你手上。
